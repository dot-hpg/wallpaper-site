Questions

Good to know

Anything else, just ask us on WhatsApp. A real person replies.

How long does the whole thing take?

About 5 to 7 days from the time you approve your mock-up. Printing takes 3 to 5 days and a single feature wall is usually installed in one day.

Will it work on my wall?

Most painted plaster walls are fine. Walls with seepage or flaking paint need to be fixed first, and we check this for free at the site visit. Damp walls are the one thing we will not wallpaper, because no adhesive holds on them.

Which material should I choose?

Matte Classic is great for bedrooms and kids' rooms. Canvas Texture is washable and hides small wall flaws, so it suits living rooms and hallways. Luxe fabric and metallic finishes are for statement walls, cafés and hotels.

Can I wipe it clean?

Canvas Texture and Luxe can be wiped with a damp cloth. Matte Classic should only be dusted. We do not recommend wallpaper inside showers or right behind a cooking hob.

I live in a rented flat. Can I still do this?

Yes. Ask for our removable adhesive installation, which comes off in panels when you move. Tell us at the site visit so we prep the wall for it.

Can I change the colours of a design?

Every design can be recoloured to match your sofa, curtains or brand colours at no extra cost. You will see the new colours on your mock-up first.

Do you deliver outside the city?

We can print and ship anywhere in India with installation instructions. Our own install team covers the city and nearby areas; ask us about larger projects elsewhere.
